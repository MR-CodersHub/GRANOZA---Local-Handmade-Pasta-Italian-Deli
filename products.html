Guida alla Cottura Perfetta

How to Cook Fresh Pasta Like an Italian Maestro

Because fresh pasta has not been dehydrated, it cooks in just 2 to 4 minutes. Follow these fundamental rules for extraordinary results.

The 10:100:1000 Ratio

For every 100g of pasta, use 1 liter (1000ml) of rolling water and 10g of coarse Mediterranean sea salt. Generous water prevents temperature drops.

Never Walk Away

Fresh ribbons need only 180 seconds. Taste a strand at the 2.5-minute mark. You want a tender yield with an imperceptible firm core ("al dente").

Liquid Gold Emulsion

Before straining, reserve half a cup of starchy water. Transfer pasta straight into your warm sauce pan and stir vigorously over low flame to bind.

Chilling & Freezing

Keep fresh trays in the coldest part of your refrigerator. To freeze, pop the whole tray in the freezer; drop directly into boiling water without thawing!
