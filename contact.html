Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about purchasing, storing, cooking, and catering our handmade Italian pasta.

Our fresh egg pasta is made without chemical preservatives and will remain in prime condition for 3 to 4 days in the coldest part of your refrigerator (34°F – 38°F). Keep it in our breathable kraft trays dusted with semolina. If you do not plan to cook it within 3 days, simply place the entire sealed tray directly into the freezer.
Yes, absolutely! In fact, you must NEVER thaw frozen fresh pasta prior to cooking, as defrosting turns the dough gummy. Drop frozen ribbons or ravioli straight into vigorously rolling salted water and simply add 60 to 90 seconds to the recommended cooking duration.
Yes. We craft bronze-extruded pastas made from 100% organic Tuscan corn and brown rice flour, as well as egg-free durum semolina orecchiette and cavatelli. Please note that while we use thoroughly sanitized dedicated extruders, our atelier handles wheat flour daily, so we do not recommend our kitchen for severe celiac disease.
For party trays serving 10 to 20 guests, we require 48 hours advance notice. For larger full-service catering banquets (30 to 150+ guests) and wedding celebrations, we recommend booking at least 1 to 2 weeks in advance to guarantee your preferred delivery window and equipment availability.
Mass-market manufacturers use teflon-coated dies because they extrude faster and don’t wear out quickly, creating slick, shiny noodles that sauce slides right off. We use traditional solid bronze dies (trafile al bronzo) and 36-hour low-temperature drying. The friction creates microscopic chalky pits and ridges, allowing sauce and starch emulsion to cling effortlessly to every single millimeter.
