Il Nostro Metodo

Four Pillars of Our Craftsmanship

From sun-drenched grain fields in Puglia to traditional bronze dies, every Granoza pasta shape follows strict centuries-old Italian culinary discipline.

01
Altamura, Puglia

100% Pugliese Semolina

Certified organic coarse durum wheat from Altamura. Amber color and high protein strength deliver the perfect al dente toothsome bite.

02
Solid Cast Bronze

Trafilatura al Bronzo

Extruded through pure solid bronze dies. Creates an artisan porous, chalky texture that clings tenaciously to every drop of sauce.

03
Beechwood Table

Hand-Rolled Sfoglia

Tender ribbon pastas and filled parcels kneaded on beechwood tables to cultivate elasticity without rubbery industrial toughness.

04
< 42°C • 36 Hours

Slow Cold Drying

Gentle static drying below 42°C for 36 hours — preserving wheat nutrients, golden aroma, and superior natural digestibility.
