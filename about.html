La Nostra Filosofia

A Passion Born Between Bologna & Bari

In Italy, pasta is not merely nourishment — it is a spiritual rhythm. It marks Sunday gatherings, seals friendships, and honors centuries of culinary memory passed down from mother to daughter without written manuals.

When Maestro Giancarlo established our atelier in 1998, he brought a set of heavy wooden rolling pins (mattarelli) hand-carved from Emilian pearwood and an uncompromising pledge: never use artificial preservatives, never rush the cold air-drying cycle, and never compromise on whole grains.

Il Nostro Cammino

Our Journey Through Generations

How an apprentice's passion in an alleyway in Bologna blossomed into a beloved neighborhood pastificio and gourmet Italian deli.

1992 Bologna Quadrilatero

Apprenticeship in Bologna

At age 19, Giancarlo De Luca apprenticed under Master Sfoglina Signora Rosa in Bologna's historic Quadrilatero district, learning the exacting art of rolling dough so thin it could be held up to the sunlight without breaking.

1998 The First Bottega

Opening the Neighborhood Bottega

Granoza opened its doors with two wooden tables, a small counter, and a single vintage bronze extrusion press brought across the Atlantic from Treviso. Neighbors gathered each morning lured by the aroma of freshly kneaded semolina.

2010 D.O.P. Partnerships

Expanding to an Authentic Salumeria

To accompany fresh pasta, we partnered directly with family dairies in Parma and salumifici in Modena to install our cold cuts counter, offering prosciutto di Parma aged on chestnut racks and cave-ripened pecorino.

Today Generational Atelier

The Tradition Continues

Today, the second generation of the De Luca family works side-by-side with our master sfoglini, providing daily fresh pasta to hundreds of neighborhood families, renowned trattorias, and festive catering banquets.

Tecniche Artigianali

Honoring Ancient Methods

Industrial pasta factories prioritize speed and volume. We prioritize touch, patience, and the physics of dough structure.

Rough Beechwood Friction

Manual Sfoglia Rolling

Rolling on rough wood aerates the dough and imparts microscopic friction channels across the sheet, giving fresh tagliatelle and pappardelle their velvety, sauce-binding grip.

100% Solid Bronze
Trafilatura al Bronzo

Solid Bronze Extrusion Dies

Bronze dies leave a chalky, pale matte exterior on macaroni and penne. When simmered, this microscopic roughness releases natural starch that turns pasta water into silk.

< 40°C • 36 Hours
Enzyme Protection

Static Low-Heat Air Curing

We dry our non-fresh pasta slowly on breathable cedar frames at ambient room temperatures (under 40°C), preserving the wheat's natural nutty aroma and delicate enzymes.

Provenienza e Origine

Traceable Sourcing Across Italy's Regions

We never buy generic commodity grains or mystery bulk oils. Every sack of durum semolina, wheel of cheese, and leg of salumi is traced back to a specific Italian cooperative or family estate.

Altamura Durum Wheat Semolina (Puglia)

100% certified coarse-milled durum wheat grown under intense Mediterranean sunshine, providing natural yellow pigment and superior gluten elasticity.

Pasture-Raised Hen Eggs (Local Valley Farms)

Laid by pasture-roaming hens fed on natural grains and alfalfa, producing brilliant amber yolks that give our egg noodles their rich, buttery hue.

30-Month Parmigiano Reggiano (Parma Hills)

Crafted exclusively from raw milk of heritage Vacche Rosse cattle. Tested and wheel-branded by the Parmigiano Reggiano Consortium.
